QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Is this only for computer science graduates?

No — the skill taxonomy covers technical, soft, digital-literacy, and industry-readiness skills across fields, and the job postings span multiple industries.

How is my employability score calculated?

A model trained on graduate outcome data weighs your CGPA, skills, projects, internships, certifications, and experience to produce a score and risk level.

Can universities see individual student data?

University and researcher accounts see aggregated analytics, not individual graduate profiles, unless a graduate explicitly shares their data.

Is it free to create a graduate account?

Yes. Graduate accounts are free to create and use.
